# White Cup

## Register

brand

## Users

Residents and visitors of Samara looking for specialty coffee, an all-day breakfast, a calm meeting place, or a small cultural event. They often arrive from a phone and need to understand the cafe's character, choose the right location, see what is available, and open a route in one or two taps.

## Product Purpose

The page turns White Cup's atmosphere into a trustworthy first visit: show the warm cafe, explain the two locations and their different rhythms, expose the menu and current event direction, then move visitors to a menu, route, phone call, or public social feed. Success is a page that feels as considered as the cafe and never invents operational facts.

## Brand Personality

Warm, curious, hand-made. The voice is welcoming and concrete, with a little Samara sketchbook charm. The page should feel like a real place where coffee, architecture, food and people overlap.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS cards, glassmorphism, neon gradients, stock-photo sameness, pure white/black, copied map embeds that hide the entrance, unreadable handwritten body text, repeated desktop columns on mobile, and synthetic cafe scenes presented as documentary photography.

## Design Principles

1. Show the place before explaining it: real photographic evidence carries the first impression.
2. Keep the sketchbook voice consistent: paper, ink, orange marks, Samara line art, and organic cuts repeat with restraint.
3. Make the next visit obvious: menu, route, phone and fresh events are always reachable.
4. Prefer verified, editable facts over clever but stale copy.
5. Make mobile feel authored, not compressed: preserve the invariants while changing the composition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for text, controls, keyboard access and motion. Use readable body sans, descriptive image alternatives, real links, visible focus, 44px targets, a focus-managed mobile menu, and a complete reduced-motion mode. Keep all meaningful content available without JavaScript.
